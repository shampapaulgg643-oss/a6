Our story

A small website about a small, important thing

Alpaca Footprint is an independent guide for anyone who wants warmer, drier, happier feet, written by people who have learned the hard way.

How it began

Cold feet, then a curious discovery

It started on a chilly hiking trip, with soaked cotton socks and miserable feet. A friend lent a spare pair of alpaca-blend socks, and the difference was immediate: warm, dry and comfortable all day. That made us curious. Why did fibre matter so much? Why did some socks last for years while others wore through in months?

We started reading, asking knitters and outdoor guides, and testing socks on long walks and cold evenings. Alpaca Footprint is where we share what we have learned.

Independent

We do not sell socks and are not affiliated with any brand. Our only goal is useful, honest information.

Practical

Every tip is something you can use: how to choose, fit, wash, store or mend a pair.

Respectful of animals

We care about how fibre animals are raised and encourage readers to look for transparent, responsible sourcing.

How we write

Simple, accurate and tested

Our guides draw on textile references, information from fibre organisations and conversations with knitters, mill workers and outdoor enthusiasts. Care advice is tried on real socks before we recommend it.

We avoid health claims and stick to comfort, fit and fibre facts. Read more in our Editorial Policy.

Get involved

Share your sock wisdom

Have a care trick, a darning technique or a question we have not answered? We love hearing from readers, and many of our best tips started as messages in our inbox.
