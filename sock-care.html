Care guide

Wash, dry, store, repeat: the sock care guide

Good socks can last for years with a little care. Here is how to wash, dry, store and even mend them, fibre by fibre.

Washing

The gentle wash routine

  1. Turn inside out

    This protects the outer surface and lets the wash reach sweat and skin cells inside.

  2. Choose a cool cycle

    Use a wool, delicate or cold cycle for alpaca and merino. Hot water and heavy agitation can felt animal fibres.

  3. Use mild detergent

    A wool-safe detergent without enzymes or bleach is best. Skip fabric softener.

  4. Use a mesh bag

    Keeping socks together in a wash bag stops pairs going missing and reduces friction.

Drying & storage

Let them breathe

  • Reshape socks while damp and lay them flat or hang them by the toe.
  • Keep them away from radiators and direct strong sunlight, which can weaken fibres and fade colours.
  • Store pairs loosely folded rather than balled up tightly, which stretches the cuffs.
  • For long-term storage of wool socks, use a clean, dry drawer and a natural deterrent such as cedar blocks to discourage moths.
Repair

Don’t bin them: darn them

A small hole at the heel or toe does not have to be the end of a favourite pair. Darning, an old technique of weaving new yarn across a worn area, can add years of life to good wool socks.

  1. Catch it early

    Thin patches are easier to reinforce than full holes.

  2. Use matching yarn

    A similar weight of wool or alpaca yarn blends in and wears evenly.

  3. Weave, don’t pull

    Stitch rows in one direction, then weave across them. Keep tension relaxed so it stays comfortable.
