Editorial Policy

How we research, write and correct our sock guides.

Last updated: October 1, 2026

We want every guide on Alpaca Footprint to be useful, accurate and fair. These are the standards we follow.

Independence

We do not accept payment or free products in exchange for coverage and we do not promote specific brands. If advertising appears on the site, advertisers have no say in our content.

Research

We use textile references, information published by fibre and wool organisations, and practical experience from knitters, outdoor guides and our own testing. Where information is uncertain or varies by product, we say so.

No health claims

We write about comfort, warmth, fit and care. We do not claim that any fibre or sock prevents, treats or cures medical conditions, and we encourage readers with foot concerns to seek professional advice.

Animal welfare

When writing about alpacas and other fibre animals, we aim to be accurate and respectful, and we encourage readers to look for transparent information about how fibre is sourced.

Corrections

If you spot an error, please let us know through our contact page. We review every report and correct confirmed mistakes promptly, updating the page date for significant changes.

Images and tools

Photographs come from Unsplash under the Unsplash License and are described with accurate alternative text. Diagrams and the size finder are created by us and are clearly presented as general guides.
